## Scoring protocols

- **Boxes:** one-to-one greedy IoU matching, group rule (`score_image`, `either`), reported at IoU ≥ 0.5 and ≥ 0.3, pooled over photos. Main row without ignore regions. With `--use-ignore` (supplementary): an unmatched prediction with ≥ 50 % of its area inside one ignore region is dropped, and ignore regions never count as FN. Labeling rule: a balcony has a railing / balustrade, or a solid parapet that projects from the wall. Sills, ledges, flower boxes and flush panels are not balconies. One box per section of a railing split by piers; the box covers railing plus slab.
- **Classifier:** scored only on detected crops that match a GT box (IoU ≥ 0.5). A triangle floor counts as rectangle. Material is scored on open_work crops and floor on floor-labeled crops.
- **Balcony attributes, default since 2026-11-07 (user): conditional, no double penalty.** Material is scored only where the truth and the prediction are both open work, floor only where both are projecting. A wrong kind or structure is counted once, in its own head, and takes the crop out of the dependent head's denominator (so n differs per stage; always report n). The "vote effect" (fixed / broken) uses the crops that pass the condition at both stages. Report the four heads and their mean (macro F1 and accuracy). Implemented as `head_metrics(..., conditional=True)` in `balcony_pipeline/score_test100_types_final.py` (also used by `score_val30_types.py`); `--unconditional` gives the old rule (material with a wrong kind and floor with a wrong structure count as wrong). Numbers cited before 2026-11-07 use the old rule. Floor tie rule (user, 2026-11-07; `enclosed.vote_open_floor_shape(shapes, probs)`: the top shape wins, a tie goes to the higher mean classifier floor probability; before, circle / triangle needed more votes than each other shape and ties went to rectangle). test100, conditional scoring, structure by depth (`eval_test100_final_facade_v4_{k1w2,k1w2links}_depth_floortie_20261107.md`), floor macro F1 / acc after the vote: default clustering 0.819 / 0.900 → 0.820 / 0.895, `--cluster-links` 0.788 / 0.907 → 0.793 / 0.897 (no vote 0.895 / 0.943).
